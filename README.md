Customer shopping behaviour Analysis Using Power BI

 Project Overview

The Customer shopping behaviour Analysis project uses Microsoft Power BI to analyze retail purchasing data and identify meaningful business insights.
The project explores product and brand performance, customer purchasing behavior, festive sales, delivery efficiency, payment preferences, and customer satisfaction.
An interactive five-page dashboard enables users to explore retail performance using KPIs, charts, matrices, and slicers.

 Insights

*Product Performance: Footwear generates the highest purchase amount among the three categories, while Accessories contribute the least.

*Brand Performance: Nike leads in purchase amount, followed by Puma and Adidas.

*Customer Satisfaction: The average review rating is 3.48, indicating potential opportunities to improve customer satisfaction.

*Customer Behaviour: Low-frequency customers form the largest customer-frequency group, presenting an opportunity to encourage repeat purchases.

*Festive Sales: Standard Day contributes the highest purchase amount among the displayed occasions.

*Payment Preferences: UPI is the most frequently used payment method

*Delivery Performance: Standard delivery has a higher average delivery time than Express delivery.

*Product Returns: Approximately 2K returned transactions are displayed, making return analysis an important area for further investigation.

 Recommendations

1. Improve Product Sales: Maintain sufficient stock of high-performing products and introduce targeted promotions for lower-performing categories
2. Optimise Brand Performance: Analyse successful brands and identify opportunities to improve the performance of weaker brands.
3. Enhance Customer Satisfaction: Investigate low product ratings and customer feedback to identify areas for improvement.
4. Increase Customer Retention: Introduce loyalty programmes and personalised offers to encourage repeat purchases.
5. Strengthen Festive Marketing: Plan promotions using historical sales trends and compare festive performance with regular-day sales.
6. Improve Payment Convenience: Ensure reliable UPI transactions while continuing to support alternative payment methods.
7. Optimise Delivery Operations: Monitor delivery times and shipping costs to identify opportunities for greater efficiency.
8. Reduce Product Returns: Analyse return patterns by product, category, and brand to identify potential quality or delivery issues.

 Conclusion

The Customer Shopping Behaviour Analysis project uses Power BI to transform shopping data into meaningful business insights through interactive dashboards. It analyzes product and brand performance, purchase quantities, promotional sales, customer demographics, subscription patterns, payment methods, delivery performance, and product returns. The insights help identify popular products, understand customer preferences, evaluate sales patterns on regular and promotional days, and assess delivery efficiency. The project demonstrates how data analytics and visualization can support informed business decisions, improve marketing strategies, optimize inventory management, and enhance customer satisfaction.
